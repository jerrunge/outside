How the scoring works

Each trip carries five bars, left to right in your priority order. Rust bars are the general criteria; the teal bar is fishing, weighted toward wild trout over stocked. Skinny-dip is a tiebreaker, not a filter — it's flagged where the water and the solitude both deliver.
